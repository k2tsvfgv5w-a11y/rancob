Coming to Google Play · in closed testing

Field experiments, tidy from design to analysis.

Rancob is an Android app for agronomy students and researchers. Generate a randomized plot layout, record observations in the field without signal, check your data, and get ANOVA with mean-separation letters ready for your report. The app is available in Indonesian and English.

Features

Randomized layouts

Completely randomized (CRD) and randomized complete block (RCBD) designs, reproducible from a stored seed.

Factorials

Two or three factors, with every main effect and interaction up to A×B×C.

Schedules by DAP

Each parameter has its own schedule in days after planting; sessions are built automatically.

Offline recording

Per plot and per sample, with photos and notes, all stored on the phone.

Data checks

Missing values, outliers and Shapiro-Wilk normality of residuals before analysis.

ANOVA and post-hoc tests

Duncan (DMRT), Tukey (HSD) and LSD at 5% or 1%, with letter notation.

Field journal

Land preparation, planting, treatments, fertilizing, spraying and harvest, with a pre-harvest interval warning.

Export and backup

CSV export, and ZIP backups with photos to move an experiment to another phone.

Sync and collaboration soon

With Pro: sync across devices and invite viewers or editors. Collaborators join free.

Try it in your browser beta

Rancob Web designs the layout, takes your data and runs ANOVA right in the browser, with the same engine as the Android app. Data stays in that browser (no account); sync, collaboration, photos and the field journal are not available on the web. Same limit as the Free plan: 1 active experiment.
